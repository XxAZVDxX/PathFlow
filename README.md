PathFlow is an iPhone travel aid for blind and low-vision people. It brings spoken descriptions of nearby surroundings, walking directions, and Hong Kong bus information together in one app.

## What PathFlow can help with

- **Ask about what's ahead.** Use your voice to ask for a description of the scene shown by the camera. Descriptions may be incomplete or incorrect.
- **Find your way on foot.** Search for a destination, view the route on a map, and follow navigation instructions. Routes are shown in the map interface, not drawn over the live camera view.
- **Check Hong Kong bus information.** Find nearby bus stops and check available routes and estimated arrival times. Information depends on your location and the availability of transport data.
- **Get optional LiDAR distance cues.** On compatible iPhones with LiDAR, PathFlow can estimate the distance ahead and provide threat-level, haptic, or spoken cues. These estimates are not a guarantee that an area is safe or clear.
- **Use accessibility features.** PathFlow supports VoiceOver, voice input, and spoken guidance.

## Device compatibility

PathFlow requires an iPhone running iOS 18.2 or later. LiDAR distance cues are available only on compatible iPhone models with LiDAR. Camera, microphone, and location access are needed for the related features.

## Please travel safely

PathFlow is an assistive tool, not a replacement for a white cane, guide dog, safe travel decisions, or accessible infrastructure. AI descriptions and distance estimates can be wrong or miss hazards. Always use your usual mobility aids and take care when travelling.
